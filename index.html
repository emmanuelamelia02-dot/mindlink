LOW MOOD & DEPRESSION SUPPORT

Small steps that may help

Depression is not a personal failure, and an app cannot diagnose or cure it. These steps can support recovery while you seek human or professional help.

1️⃣
Tell one trusted personLet a friend, family member, counsellor or teacher know that you have been struggling.
2️⃣
Make the next step very smallTry drinking water, showering, eating something simple, opening the curtains or taking a five-minute walk.
3️⃣
Keep a basic routineA regular wake time, meals, gentle movement and sleep routine can give your day more structure.
4️⃣
Reduce isolationStay near supportive people, join a safe peer group or use the support chat in this app.
5️⃣
Seek professional supportSpeak with a counsellor, doctor or mental-health professional, especially when symptoms persist or affect daily life.

Urgent safety: If you might hurt yourself or cannot stay safe, contact local emergency services now and stay with a trusted person.

Important

MINDLINK can offer supportive steps, but it cannot diagnose, treat or cure depression. A counsellor, doctor or qualified mental-health professional can provide proper assessment and care.
